Privacy Policy – szokehome

Last updated: November 11, 2026

szokehome is a personal integration that connects my own Home Assistant instance to my own Google Calendar. It is used only by me.

What data is accessed

Calendar events (titles, times, locations and descriptions) from the Google account that authorizes the app.

How it is used

Events are read by my self-hosted Home Assistant to display upcoming events on my dashboard and, optionally, to run home automations.

Sharing and storage

Calendar data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. It stays within my own Home Assistant installation.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
